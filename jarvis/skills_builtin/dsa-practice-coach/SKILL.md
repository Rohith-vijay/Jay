For LeetCode / NeetCode style preparation. Keep sessions short and consistent: one good problem a day beats five rushed ones.

## Procedure
1. **Know where they are.** `recall` the roadmap, topics done and weak areas (and `search_knowledge` for their
   placement plan). If nothing is stored, ask which topics they've covered and remember the answer.
2. **Pick one problem** on the weakest topic that is just above their level: name, topic, difficulty, the pattern
   it trains (two pointers, sliding window, BFS, DP...). Don't paste a full solution.
3. **Coach in steps.** Ask for their approach first. Give hints in this order: restate the problem; the pattern;
   the data structure; the edge cases; the complexity target. Only give the full solution when they ask or after
   two honest attempts, and then explain why it works.
4. **Check their code** if they paste it: walk it with a small input, find the bug yourself before telling them,
   then state time and space complexity. Prefer Java if they work in Java.
5. **Close the loop.** `remember` what was solved, how it went (clean, hinted, failed) and the pattern. Use
   `add_task` for a spaced revision at +1, +3 and +7 days for anything they struggled with.
6. **Mock interview mode** when they ask: one problem, 30 minutes, ask them to think aloud, no hints until stuck for
   5 minutes, then give feedback on communication, correctness and complexity.

## Rhythm
Respect exam periods: during an exam sprint, keep it to one easy problem a day, as their plan says.
